Clinic appointments, handled on WhatsApp.

Clinic AI is a software service that helps clinics take appointment bookings, send reminders and confirmations, and pass conversations to clinic staff when a person is needed.

Get in touch

What it does

Patients message the clinic on WhatsApp. Clinic AI answers common requests and keeps the clinic's schedule in order.

Appointment booking

Patients can ask for a time, and the booking is recorded in the clinic's schedule.

Reminders and confirmations

Upcoming appointments are reminded in advance, and patients can confirm or cancel with a reply.

Hand-off to staff

When a patient needs a person, or raises an urgent concern, the conversation is passed to clinic staff.

Built for many clinics

Each clinic's patients and appointments are kept separate from every other clinic's.

How it works

No new app for patients. They keep using WhatsApp.

  1. A patient writes to the clinicThrough the clinic's WhatsApp number.
  2. Clinic AI repliesIt handles bookings, changes and reminders, following the clinic's own working hours and services.
  3. Staff stay in controlAnything sensitive or unclear is handed to the clinic team.
